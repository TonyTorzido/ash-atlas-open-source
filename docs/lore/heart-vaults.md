# The Ash Hearts

Every Ash Heart is a heart someone gave away because of the Ashen Wars.

The welcome crawl tells the big story: "Our fathers went to negotiate peace. They returned with war." The Ash
Heart dungeons tell small ones. Each heart belonged to one person, and each was given up for a reason the war left
them: love, grief, desire, pride, guilt. The player does not fight these people. They listen to them, finish what
they could not, and carry the heart out.

Tone: very sad, never cruel. Short plain lines, the same voice as the welcome crawl. No villains among the heart
keepers, only people the war broke.

## The Tilted Vault: Pell Ashgrave (love)

Shipped content. Room lines live in `apps/client/src/heartPuzzles/vault/vaultRooms.ts` (`lore`, plus
`solvedLore` for the Heart room). They show at the top of Old Pell's popup, before the room instructions and
Start/Leave.

### Story

- Pell Ashgrave was a stonemason in a canyon town during the Ashen Wars.
- His wife was Maren. His daughter Wren collected round stones she called her marbles.
- He built Wren a little wooden maze she could tilt to roll the marbles home.
- The war came down the canyon in the night. The only way out was the old sealed quarry vault, which opens only
  for a living heart given freely.
- Pell carved out his heart and set it in the door. The floors tilted open. Maren carried Wren through while the
  town burned.
- The vault kept his heart beating in stone, and it kept him.
- As they went, Wren pressed a marble into his hand: "Roll it home, Papa. We'll come back."
- He has been rolling it home ever since. The tilting floors are her maze, rebuilt in stone.
- Every time footsteps come down the stairs, he thinks it is them.

### The Ember Shades

The Ember Shades in the combat halls are townsfolk who did not reach the door before the vault sealed: the baker,
the Tolly boys, old Ansel who sharpened Pell's chisels. They still wait by the door. Pell grieves them and asks the
player not to hate them.

### Room by room

| Room | Pell talks about |
| --- | --- |
| I. The Marble Maze | Wren and her marbles, the wooden maze, why the floor tilts. |
| II. Two Weights | The night of the fire. The shades were his neighbours. The heart in the door. Maren's hand slipping from his. Wren's marble and her promise. |
| III. The Turning Floor | He has counted every season. Wren would be grown, Maren grey. They are probably gone. The shades knock some nights. He still thinks every footstep is them. |
| IV. The Heart Chamber, before Start | This is the last room. Finishing lets the vault let go of him, and then no one will be here when they come back. He is afraid to finish. |
| IV. The Heart Chamber, after the solve | "They're not coming back, are they. ...No. They lived. That was the point. That was always the point." He gives the heart: "If you ever meet a woman named Wren with a pocket full of stones... tell her Papa rolled it home." |

The farewell opens by itself once the Heart room's seal is confirmed, after the "The Heart stirs" celebration.
Talking to Pell again shows it again.

## Future hearts

Four more Ash Heart dungeons are planned. Only names, keepers and the shape of each story are decided.

### Rimehold: Mott (grief)

Mott's son died in the war. Mott could not bear to feel it, so he froze his own heart. Rimehold is everything
around him gone still and cold with it. The player thaws what he locked away.

### Hush Bog: Liesl (desire)

Liesl gave her heart to a war-born thing with a beautiful voice. It sang to her from the bog after the fighting
ended, and she followed. The bog is quiet now because it has her heart, and it does not need to sing any more.

### Starfall Observatory: Orla (pride)

Orla traded her heart to the stars to see the war before it came. She saw it. She warned everyone. No one believed
her. She is still at the telescope, still certain, still alone.

### Upturned Temple: Tikka (guilt)

Tikka was one of the fathers who went to negotiate peace and returned with war, the generation the welcome crawl
blames. He gave his heart away under the weight of that. The temple is turned upside down the way his world was.

## Rules for new heart stories

- The heart was given away because of the Ashen Wars. Always.
- The keeper is a person, not a monster. The player helps them finish something.
- Combat-room enemies, where there are any, belong to the keeper's story (like the Ember Shades) and the keeper
  mourns them.
- One emotion per heart. Say it plainly. Let the last room hurt.
