## 2. Mob into player: hits to kill by tier and armour (v4)

Owner rule (2026-10-21): a mob's base hit comes from its tier, tuned so an equal-level hit at matrix
multiplier 1.0 kills a 100 HP player in about 14 hits (low), 8 (medium) or 4 (strong) (owner 2026-10-21).
Base hit: low 7, medium 12, strong 25. The mob's damageMultiplier (bosses x1.25-x2) applies on top, then armour and the 5%-per-level rule.
Tiers were drafted from the mob value pass labels: elite or boss spawn rarity, damageMultiplier above 1, or a
miniboss -> strong; rare spawn or 9+ target hits (Hunt) -> medium; Critter and Game -> low.
